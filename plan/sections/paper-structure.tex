\section{Paper Structure (Phase 1: NLI Assignment)}

The assignment paper will follow this structure (modeled after the original paper's format):

\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
    \item \textbf{Abstract} --- 150-200 words summarizing the adaptation
    \item \textbf{Introduction} --- Textual Entailment task, motivation for Hindi, gaps in original work
    \item \textbf{Related Work} --- Original paper, XLM-RoBERTa, XNLI, cross-lingual NLI
    \item \textbf{Dataset} --- XNLI Hindi: size, sources, label distribution, sample pairs
    \item \textbf{Methodology} --- XLM-RoBERTa architecture, fine-tuning procedure, hyperparameters
    \item \textbf{Experiments \& Results} --- Accuracy, precision, recall, F1; comparison table with original
    \item \textbf{Conclusion \& Future Work} --- Summary, limitations, directions (dialectal Hindi, other Indic languages)
    \item \textbf{References} --- 6-8 key citations
\end{enumerate}
