\section{Proposed Approach}

\subsection{Overview}

We will build a Textual Entailment system for Hindi by fine-tuning \textbf{XLM-RoBERTa}, a multilingual transformer pre-trained on 100 languages, on the \textbf{XNLI dataset}.

\subsection{Why XLM-RoBERTa?}

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item Pre-trained on 100 languages including Hindi and Urdu
    \item State-of-the-art on cross-lingual NLP benchmarks
    \item Achieved +9.2\% accuracy improvement for Urdu on XNLI over previous models (Conneau et al., 2019)
    \item No language identification needed --- model infers language automatically
    \item Available in base (125M params) and large (355M params) variants
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Why XNLI Dataset?}

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item 5,000 test pairs and 2,500 validation pairs per language
    \item Professionally translated from English MultiNLI by human translators
    \item Covers 14 diverse text genres (fiction, telephone speech, letters, 9/11 report, etc.)
    \item Three labels: Entailment, Neutral, Contradiction --- richer than the original paper's binary setup
    \item Freely available on Hugging Face Datasets
\end{itemize}
