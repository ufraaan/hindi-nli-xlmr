\section{Executive Summary}

This document outlines a \textbf{two-phase plan} spanning 8 weeks.

\textbf{Phase 1 (Weeks 1--3):} Adapt the research paper \textit{``Textual Entailment for Arabic Language Based on Lexical and Semantic Matching''} (Khader et al., 2016) for a Deep Learning course assignment. The original paper achieves 61\% precision/recall on Arabic textual entailment using hand-crafted lexical features. We adapt it to Hindi using modern transformer-based deep learning (XLM-RoBERTa fine-tuned on XNLI), targeting 85\%+ accuracy.

\textbf{Phase 2 (Weeks 4--8):} Write a technical review paper on \textbf{Knowledge Graph Construction for Hindi / Indic Languages}, following the structure of the Arabic KG survey (Ahmed et al., 2022) as a template. This will be a supervised undergraduate publication with guidance from the course professor.
