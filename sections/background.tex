\section{Background}

\subsection{The Original Paper (Khader et al., 2016)}

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item \textbf{Task:} Recognizing Textual Entailment (RTE) for Arabic
    \item \textbf{Approach:} Lexical analysis (word overlap + bigram matching) + semantic matching (synonyms)
    \item \textbf{Dataset:} ArbTEDS --- 618 Text-Hypothesis pairs
    \item \textbf{Results:} 61\% precision and recall (Entails: 68\% precision, NotEntails: 58\% precision)
    \item \textbf{Tools:} Python, ISRI stemmer, MSTParser, Mada POS tagger
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Limitations of the Original Work}

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
    \item Manual feature engineering --- does not scale to new domains
    \item Simple threshold-based decision --- no learning involved
    \item Small dataset (618 pairs) --- limits generalization
    \item Binary classification (Entails / NotEntails) --- no neutral/contradiction distinction
    \item The authors themselves note in their future work that testing on other languages is needed
\end{itemize}
